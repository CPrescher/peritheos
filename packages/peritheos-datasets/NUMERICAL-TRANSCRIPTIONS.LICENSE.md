# Scope of numerical-transcription redistribution assessments

Reviewed 5 November 2026. The installed `redistribution-review.json` identifies
each exact declaration/resource, source reference, table/figure locator,
content type, decision, rationale and unresolved qualifications. The
`manifest.json` policy distinguishes existing source-declaration admissions
from the new, separately assessed numerical resources. These are maintainer
redistribution decisions, not legal opinions or Debian acceptance.

## What is admitted

The assessed resources contain scientific numerical values, reported errors,
ordinary phase/run/source labels, quantitative digitization coordinates or
identified model outputs. They use normalized scientific columns. Contributor
diagnostic comments remain distinguishable from published observations.
Article prose, source artwork, captions, original workbook layouts, publisher
PDFs and author software are not admitted by this review.

The factual-content assessments distinguish individual values from protected
expression and independently protected compilations/databases. They do not
claim that every scientific table is unprotected, that all experimental data
are exempt from database rights, or that a particular row count guarantees
an insubstantial extraction. The identified large collections, heterogeneous
compilations and known NC/ND cases remain held as
listed in the review. Source access and scientific verification are not grants.

## Rights and attribution remain separate

Existing contributor CC0 1.0 dedications cover only the rights explicitly
held and dedicated by those contributors in transcription, normalization,
metadata or arrangement. This notice does not create missing contributor
grants or dedicate rights held by a publisher, original author or database maker.
**Unspecified source terms remain unspecified.** Admission under a factual
assessment is not a new source license and does not relabel a publication CC0
or CC BY. Retain the original references, source terms, normalizations and
measurement/digitization/calculation distinctions in each declaration.

The named Zenodo 3747000 workbooks retain their verified source CC BY 4.0
grant. The Bykova author export retains its separately recorded author-held
CC BY 4.0 grant; the identified Cernok-derived numerical anchor is assessed
separately as a numerical fit result. The author declaration is not extended
to unrelated literature rights. Existing use/recommendation qualifications
are scientific limitations and are not removed by redistribution admission.

## Limits for downstream distributors

The assessment inspected the preserved numerical resources and contributor
provenance. It did not freshly verify every original publication's numerical
transcription, authenticate every permission claim or audit acquisition
contracts. Copyright/database rights and contractual restrictions can require
separate analysis. A valid third-party restriction is not overridden by this
notice. Downstream reviewers can identify and exclude assessed resources
separately from source-license admissions using the installed ledger.

Legal/source framework and source-specific decisions are documented in the
repository report `docs/research/numerical-dataset-redistribution.md` and the
installed review. Original evidence remains unchanged and traceable.
