# LitCurate source-exhaustion audit: citation and compilation papers

Audit date: 2026-09-06.


This audit closes the extracting-paper families left after the production and
model-incompleteness audits. The governing rule is source ownership: an EOS
quoted in a comparison table, adopted as a thermodynamic input, or summarized
in a review is not reissued under the extracting paper's DOI. It must instead
be audited under the primary publication that generated the measurements or
calculation. A row also cannot become executable by borrowing a missing
reference volume or derivative from a different publication.

The LitCurate export labels 118 of the 122 rows below `Citation-reported`,
three `Unspecified`, and one `Source-reported`. The single source-labelled row
is the AIP proceedings version of Gong et al.'s (Mg0.92Fe0.08)SiO3 shock
result; the same study is already represented by the accepted, data-backed
record under its primary GRL DOI
[10.1029/2003GL019132](https://doi.org/10.1029/2003GL019132). It is therefore a
publication duplicate, not another independent EOS.

Result: **0 new production records; all 122 candidates explicitly disposed.**

## Audit conclusions

- The 1990–2007 lower-mantle thermodynamic and seismological papers use
  externally published mineral properties as model inputs or comparison
  values. Their rows remain useful provenance pointers but are not new EOS
  measurements.
- The MgO and ferropericlase methodological papers similarly compile pressure
  standards, acoustic moduli, or earlier compression fits. Where a reference
  volume is absent, the row is additionally non-executable.
- The 2020–2025 papers are sound-velocity studies or reviews. Their quoted EOS
  values support density/velocity calculations but retain the DOI and sample
  lineage of the older primary study.
- The two MgGeO3 values in the 2025 texture paper trace to the already audited
  primary compression studies; the post-perovskite value is already present
  from Kubo et al. (2006).
- No material card, numerical dataset, or EOS record is created from this
  audit. Reusing these rows would duplicate existing records or assign a
  secondary paper's DOI to someone else's fit.

This closes the extracting-paper scope only. If a cited underlying primary is
not already present in Peritheos, it remains eligible when independently found
and audited from its own paper and data.
