# Tsuchiya et al. (2004): MgSiO3 perovskite/post-perovskite phase transition

Primary source: T. Tsuchiya, J. Tsuchiya, K. Umemoto, and R. M. Wentzcovitch, *Earth and Planetary Science Letters* **224**, 241-248 (2004), DOI [10.1016/j.epsl.2004.05.017](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.epsl.2004.05.017).

## Audit result

- **HOLD — MgSiO3 post-perovskite 300 K EOS summary.** Table 1 gives V0 = 24.662(7) cm3 mol-1, B0 = 222(1) GPa (221(1) in the adjacent prose), and B0' = 4.2(1).
- **HOLD — MgSiO3 perovskite 300 K EOS summary.** Table 1 gives V0 = 24.704(4) cm3 mol-1, B0 = 248(1) GPa, and B0' = 3.91(1).

The table caption and Section 3 state that the quoted uncertainties include the range obtained by fitting both a third-order finite-strain EOS and a Vinet EOS. The paper does not publish either model's individual coefficient set or the calculated P-V rows. The central summaries cannot safely be encoded as either BM3 or Vinet without inventing the equation identity, so neither phase is a production record.
