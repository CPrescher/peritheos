# Koemets et al. (2023) Fe-rich silicate perovskites

## Source and candidate resolution

The primary source is Koemets et al., *Crystal chemistry and compressibility of
Fe0.5Mg0.5Al0.5Si0.5O3 and FeMg0.5Si0.5O3 silicate perovskites at pressures up
to 95 GPa*, *Frontiers in Chemistry* **11**, 1258389 (2023),
[doi:10.3389/fchem.2023.1258389](https://doi.org/10.3389/fchem.2023.1258389).
The audit used the open-access publisher article and official Supplementary
Tables S1--S3. No digitization was required.

## Accepted Pnma branch

The composition already has a Peritheos material card. Its `Pbnm` setting and
the article's `Pnma` setting are equivalent descriptions of space group 62, so
the accepted record extends that card rather than creating a duplicate. The
sample was synthesized in a Kawai-type multi-anvil press and measured at 293 K
by single-crystal synchrotron XRD in neon. The selected pressures were derived
from the neon scale of Fei et al. (2007); ruby fluorescence was used only below
6 GPa and therefore does not calibrate the selected branch.

All 18 Pnma refinements from Supplementary Table S3 are bundled in
`fa50-bridgmanite-koemets-2023-supplement-table3.csv`. The measurements at 7.5
and 8.6 GPa lie inside the source's reported `8 +/- 2 GPa` transformation
interval. They are preserved with zero fit flags. The phase-specific EOS uses
the remaining 16 rows from 10.8 to 60.0 GPa.

The composition-specific Figure 4 caption reports the BM2 result
`V0 = 171.2(2) A^3`, `K0 = 221(3) GPa`; a later general sentence prints
`212(3) GPa`. The table data and plotted composition-specific curve support
221 GPa, so the record treats 212 GPa as a source-side transposition rather
than silently averaging the two values.

## Numerical reproduction and limitations

The deterministic reproduction is
`scripts/reproduce_koemets_2023_fa50_bridgmanite.py`. On the 16 selected rows,
the published curve has a pressure RMSE of `0.677631 GPa` and maximum absolute
residual of `1.404195 GPa`. An errors-in-variables fit using the printed
pressure and volume uncertainties gives
`V0 = 171.306945(222) A^3` and `K0 = 220.0083(3.0285) GPa`; both reproduce the
published coefficients within their quoted uncertainties. An unweighted
pressure-space diagnostic gives `V0 = 171.305848 A^3` and `K0 = 220.020975
GPa`.

The source does not publish coefficient covariance, confidence convention,
fit weights, fit software, or row-wise neon volumes. Exact pressure
recalculation is therefore unavailable. The held R3c and P21/n records can be
implemented later once their deposited structure files are retrieved and
audited; no coordinates or site occupancies were fabricated for this batch.
