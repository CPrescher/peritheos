# Lakshtanov et al. (2007): ambient elasticity of Al,H-bearing stishovite

Primary source: D. L. Lakshtanov, K. D. Litasov, S. V. Sinogeikin, H. Hellwig, J. Li, E. Ohtani, and J. D. Bass, *American Mineralogist* **92**, 1026–1030 (2007), DOI [10.2138/am.2007.2294](https://doi.org/10.2138/am.2007.2294).

## Audit result

This paper reports ambient-condition X-ray diffraction, Brillouin scattering, and Raman measurements. Its aggregate moduli are calculated from acoustic velocities; no pressure-volume compression curve is fitted.

- **REJECT — LitCurate source row 644.** Sample K324 (6.07(5) wt% Al2O3, 0.24(2) wt% H2O) has KS0 = 290(3) GPa, GS0 = 207(2) GPa, and density 4.16(1) g cm-3. These are ambient adiabatic elastic properties rather than EOS coefficients.
- **REJECT — LitCurate source row 645.** Sample K325 (4.37(12) wt% Al2O3, 0.29(3) wt% H2O) similarly has KS0 = 296(3) GPa, GS0 = 213(2) GPa, and density 4.21(1) g cm-3, with no compression derivative or P-V fit.
- **REJECT — LitCurate citation row 646.** The 316 GPa pure-stishovite modulus is a comparison to Weidner et al. (1982), not a source-reported EOS from Lakshtanov et al. (2007).
