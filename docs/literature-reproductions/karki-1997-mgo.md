# Karki et al. (1997) MgO EOS audit

## Primary authority

- B. B. Karki, L. Stixrude, S. J. Clark, M. C. Warren, G. J. Ackland, and J. Crain, “Structure and elasticity of MgO at high pressure,” *American Mineralogist* **82** (1997), 51–60, DOI [10.2138/am-1997-1-207](https://doi.org/10.2138/am-1997-1-207).
- Open primary [RRUFF archive PDF](https://www.rruff.net/doclib/am/vol82/AM82_51.pdf), SHA-256 `4f1629bd09d1cc236d9b98bc096a82e70bc9d400cd1187f5a47dbbea3404ce3a`.
- Checked locations: methods, Equation (11), the Equation of state subsection, Table 1, and Figure 1.

The paper reports static LDA pressure-volume calculations for B1 MgO through 150 GPa. It fits the same calculated states with a fourth-order Birch-Murnaghan equation and, as a model-order sensitivity check, a third-order Birch-Murnaghan equation. These are two genuine source-reported parameterizations rather than experimental-run splits.

The LitCurate rows omitted the reference volume, and the fourth-order row omitted `K0''`. The primary source resolves both omissions: Table 1 gives `a0 = 4.2506 A`, hence the conventional B1-cell volume is `V0 = a0^3 = 76.79814209021602 A3` with `Z = 4`; the text gives `K0'' = -0.026 GPa^-1`. No uncertainties or covariance are reported.

## Numerical reproduction

`scripts/reproduce_karki_1997_mgo.py` independently implements the source's Equation (11) and standard BM3 equation, evaluates both serialized records at seven `V/V0` anchors from 1.0 to 0.7, and checks inverse pressure-volume round trips. Figure 1 plots the calculated states but does not tabulate them, so no false-precision pseudo-observations are manufactured from the plot.
