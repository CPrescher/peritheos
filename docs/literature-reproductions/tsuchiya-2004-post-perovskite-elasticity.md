# Tsuchiya et al. (2004): MgSiO3 post-perovskite elasticity

Primary source: T. Tsuchiya, J. Tsuchiya, K. Umemoto, and R. M. Wentzcovitch, *Geophysical Research Letters* **31**, L14603 (2004), DOI [10.1029/2004GL020278](https://doi.org/10.1029/2004GL020278).

## Audit result

- **REJECT — LitCurate source row 459.** The paper's Table 1 reports the 0 and 120 GPa elastic tensors and Voigt-Reuss-Hill aggregate moduli, not a fitted pressure-volume EOS. Its prose quotes B0 = 231 GPa and B' = 4.3, but explicitly attributes the pressure derivative and underlying equation-of-state calculation to the separate Tsuchiya et al. phase-transition paper. V0 and an executable equation identity are absent here, so assigning these numbers to this DOI would be a citation-row misattribution.
- **REJECT — LitCurate source row 460.** The quoted 257 GPa perovskite comparison is also attributed to prior work (Wentzcovitch et al. 2004) and has neither V0 nor an EOS identity in this paper.

The underlying Tsuchiya et al. phase-transition source, DOI 10.1016/j.epsl.2004.05.017, was audited separately. It reports complete 300 K reference volumes and modulus summaries for both phases, but its uncertainties explicitly span two different fits—third-order finite strain and Vinet—without publishing the individual coefficient sets. Those central values therefore remain on hold rather than being assigned arbitrarily to one executable family.
