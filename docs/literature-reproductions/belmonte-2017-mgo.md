# Belmonte (2017) MgO EOS audit

## Primary source and scope

- Primary paper: Donato Belmonte, “First Principles Thermodynamics of Minerals at HP-HT Conditions: MgO as a Prototypical Material,” *Minerals* **7** (2017), 183, DOI [10.3390/min7100183](https://doi.org/10.3390/min7100183).
- Open primary article: [publisher HTML](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-163X/7/10/183).
- Checked locations: computational method, Sections 3.1 and 3.3, Tables 1, 2, and 4, and Figure 7.
- Scope: all seven LitCurate rows carrying this DOI.

The paper separates pressure into static-lattice, zero-point, and thermal-vibrational terms. Its static pressure is a complete third-order Birch-Murnaghan equation with `V0 = 75.762 A3` per conventional B1 cell (`Z = 4`), `K0 = 167.01 GPa`, and `K0' = 3.95`. All eight unique static pressures printed in Table 4 are reproduced by that coefficient triplet to the table's 0.01 GPa precision.

The zero-point-corrected and 300 K values in Tables 1 and 2 are local thermophysical values generated by the paper's first-principles Mie-Gruneisen EOS. They are not complete standalone BM3 fits: the reported `V0` is the static reference, Table 4 has nonzero zero-point pressure at that volume, and the 300 K LitCurate row lacks an equilibrium volume. Encoding either row as BM3 would force `P(V0)=0` and thereby contradict Table 4. Peritheos also cannot reconstruct the exact full FPMG model from the paper's summarized moduli alone because it depends on the mode-resolved phonon spectrum.

## Bundled data and reproduction

`peritheos/data/datasets/mgo-belmonte-2017-table4-300k.csv` preserves Table 4's eight 300 K compression rows, including static, zero-point, thermal, and total pressure. The static column is temperature-independent and is the direct curve-validation dataset. `scripts/reproduce_belmonte_2017_mgo.py` evaluates the serialized BM3 at each printed volume ratio and requires every residual against the static column to remain below 0.005 GPa.
