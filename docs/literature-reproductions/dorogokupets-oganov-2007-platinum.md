# Dorogokupets--Oganov (2007) platinum pressure scale

## Audit result

The platinum parameterization is an exact executable reconstruction of the
published equations and rounded Table-I coefficients, but it is **not an
exactly refittable regression**. Dorogokupets and Oganov describe a two-stage,
weighted, six-material optimization without publishing the complete fitted
point inventory, row weights, covariance, or optimizer details. Replacing
those missing inputs with later compilations or points digitized from smooth
curves would create a different objective.

The primary source is P. I. Dorogokupets and A. R. Oganov, *Ruby, metals, and
MgO as alternative pressure scales: A semiempirical description of shock-wave,
ultrasonic, x-ray, and thermochemical data at high temperatures and
pressures*, Physical Review B **75**, 024115 (2007),
[doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.75.024115](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.75.024115).
The audit used the [author-hosted article PDF](https://uspex-team.org/static/file/PressureScales-PRB-2007.pdf).

## Construction traced to its inputs

The paper defines

\[
F=U_0+E(V)+F_{qh}+F_{anh}+F_{el}+F_{def},
\]

with a Vinet 298.15 K reference isotherm, two generalized-Bose and two
Einstein oscillators, the Al'tshuler volume law for the Gruneisen parameter,
intrinsic anharmonicity, an electronic term, and an independent-monovacancy
term. The defect volume exponents are structurally fixed to `f=-1` and
`h=-2`; the four oscillator multiplicities are constrained to sum to `3n`.

The fitted constraints have two stages:

1. Weighted least squares to pressure-calibration-independent measurements:
   low- and high-temperature heat capacity, relative enthalpy, volume,
   thermal expansion, adiabatic bulk modulus at zero pressure, and shock
   Hugoniot pressure calculated with Equation (15).
2. Addition of static compression measurements under trial pressure scales,
   refitting all EOS coefficients and checking consistency against
   `K_S(T)`. The final ruby scale results from the joint Al, Cu, Ta, W, Au,
   and Pt analysis.

For platinum, the recoverable source chain is:

| Constraint | Source identified by the paper | Recoverable evidence | Audit use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Static X-ray compression | Dewaele, Loubeyre, and Mezouar (2004), Table I | All 36 Pt rows are bundled | Stage-2 partial validation |
| Shock comparison | Holmes et al. (1989), Table III | All seven new Pt shots are bundled | Independent Equation-15 diagnostic |
| Shock fit input | 2001 Shock Wave Database, reference 30 | Cited snapshot and selected rows unavailable | No refit claim |
| Ultrasonic `K_S(T)` | Collard and McLellan (1992), Figure 1 | Coordinates used by the fit are not tabulated | No pseudo-data |
| Ambient thermochemistry and expansion | References 46--52 and 97 | The standard-entropy comparison is printed; Pt row selection and weights are not | Scalar validation only |

The ambient-data citation pool is itself heterogeneous: Gurvich et al.'s
*Thermodynamic Properties of Individual Substances* (vol. 4, book 2), Chase's
fourth-edition NIST--JANAF tables, Touloukian and Buyco's heat-capacity
compilation, Novikova and Touloukian et al.'s thermal-expansion compilations,
and Dubrovinsky and Saxena (1997). White and Collocott (1984) is specifically
*Heat Capacity of Reference Materials: Cu and W*, so it is not a platinum row
source. Reference 97 is Robie, Hemingway, and Fisher's
[USGS Bulletin 1452](https://doi.org/10.3133/b1452), from which the article
quotes `S_298=41.63 ± 0.21 J mol^-1 K^-1`. The article does not map the remaining
compilation rows to individual metals or disclose their weights, so this audit
does not infer a platinum observation table from the bibliography alone.

Dorogokupets and Oganov explicitly distinguish two platinum ultrasonic series
in Figure 7. Filled squares read from Collard and McLellan's Figure 1 were used
in the fit. Open squares calculated from that paper's Table-I polynomial were
not used because the two representations are internally inconsistent. The
polynomial therefore cannot defensibly substitute for the missing fitted
coordinates.

## Numerical evidence

The reproducible audit is generated by
`scripts/audit_dorogokupets_oganov_2007_platinum.py`; its complete result,
including all 28 printed Table-VI nodes, is stored in
`docs/data/dorogokupets-oganov-2007-platinum-audit.json`.

| Evidence class | Result | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| Table-VI Pt isochors | 28/28 nodes; RMSE 0.00705 GPa; maximum 0.01732 GPa | Exact reconstruction at the precision of rounded Table-I coefficients |
| Standard entropy | 41.4485 J mol^-1 K^-1 versus the paper's 41.45 | Exact independent check of the caloric Helmholtz implementation |
| Dewaele static rows on final Equation-16 ruby scale | 36 rows; published global curve RMSE 0.288 GPa | Recoverable stage-2 subset, not the global objective |
| Unweighted static-only Vinet fit | `V0=15.09849 A^3/atom`, `K0=270.8001 GPa`, `K0'=5.49763` | Demonstrates why static rows alone do not recover the global `276.07, 5.30` solution |
| Holmes shock diagnostic | 7 rows; RMSE 24.46 GPa | Independent extrapolative check only; not asserted to be the unpublished Shock Wave Database fit set |

The Holmes residuals are intentionally retained rather than optimized away.
The rows reach 653 GPa, far outside the printed Pt Table-VI domain, and the
2007 article identifies the separate Shock Wave Database as its stage-1 input.

## Implementation and thermodynamic closure

`DorogokupetsOganov2007` now exposes the full non-static Helmholtz contribution
and its derived internal energy, entropy, constant-volume heat capacity,
absolute thermal pressure, enthalpy, and Gibbs energy in both Python and Rust.
The published reference pressure remains the 298.15 K-subtracted pressure, so
existing pressure-scale behavior is unchanged.

Tests verify both independent potential identities

\[
P_{abs}=-\left(\frac{\partial F}{\partial V}\right)_T,
\qquad
C_V=\left(\frac{\partial U}{\partial T}\right)_V,
\]

native/Python parity, the printed entropy, and every Table-VI Pt pressure.

## Refit classification

The record remains `not_refittable` for the global published fit. This is a
statement about missing observations and weights, not about the executable
EOS: equations, coefficients, caloric identities, entropy, and calibration
table are exactly or rounding-level reproducible. The 36 Dewaele rows and
seven Holmes rows are preserved as explicitly qualified partial evidence.
