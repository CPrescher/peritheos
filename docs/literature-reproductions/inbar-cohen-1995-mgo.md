# Inbar and Cohen (1995): MgO thermal properties

Primary source: I. Inbar and R. E. Cohen, “High pressure effects on thermal properties of MgO,” *Geophysical Research Letters* **22**, 1533–1536 (1995), <https://doi.org/10.1029/95GL01086>. The authors' primary preprint is <https://arxiv.org/abs/mtrl-th/9503007>.

The paper reports molecular-dynamics pressure–volume results to 310 GPa and lists zero-pressure values of volume, isothermal bulk modulus, and its first and second pressure derivatives in Table 1. It does **not** identify an analytical pressure–volume EOS family or give its coefficients. Those thermodynamic derivatives cannot be assumed to be Birch–Murnaghan coefficients merely because LitCurate suggested a BM3 mapping. The underlying numerical P–V states are plotted but not tabulated. Consequently, the four source-owned temperature rows remain on hold rather than being converted into invented BM3 records.
