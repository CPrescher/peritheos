# Miyajima et al. (2025): quenched davemaoite

## Outcome

This paper contributes **zero EOS records**.  Its new result is a measured
ambient lattice parameter and electron-diffraction identification, not a
pressure-volume fit.  The useful structural measurement should not be padded
into an EOS by borrowing moduli from earlier papers.

Nobuyoshi Miyajima, Lin Wang, and Tomoo Katsura, “First Observation of
Quenched Davemaoite to Ambient Conditions: Its Electron Diffraction Pattern,”
*Geophysical Research Letters* **52**, e2025GL115280 (2025),
[doi:10.1029/2025GL115280](https://doi.org/10.1029/2025GL115280).
The audit checked the open publisher full text, the author-deposited article,
Supporting Information description, and archived TEM-data description.

The new nearly pure davemaoite domain has composition approximately
Ca0.98Al0.03Si0.99Mg0.005O3, cubic Pm-3m symmetry, `a=3.561(4) A`, and
`V=45.16(10) A3`.  The authors explicitly warn that possible residual pressure
means this cannot be treated as a directly determined zero-pressure EOS
volume.  No K0, K0', pressure series, or EOS regression is reported.

The paper itself proposes using its volume as a future fixed constraint for a
precise EOS refinement.  That is a useful future-data recommendation, but it
does not turn the present measurement into an executable EOS.
