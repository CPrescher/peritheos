# Mao et al. (1991): (Fe,Mg)SiO3 perovskites

Primary source: H. K. Mao, R. J. Hemley, Y. Fei, J. F. Shu, L. C. Chen,
A. P. Jephcoat, Y. Wu, and W. A. Bassett, “Effect of pressure, temperature,
and composition on lattice parameters and density of (Fe,Mg)SiO3-perovskites
to 30 GPa,” *Journal of Geophysical Research* **96**, 8069–8079 (1991),
<https://doi.org/10.1029/91JB00176>.

The source reports three genuine composition-specific room-temperature
isotherms. Their constrained moduli are statistically indistinguishable and
were reported jointly as `K0=261(4) GPa`, `K0'=4`, while the conventional-cell
reference volumes remain distinct: 162.49, 162.79, and 163.53 Å3 for MgSiO3,
Mg0.9Fe0.1SiO3, and Mg0.8Fe0.2SiO3. Shared elastic coefficients therefore do
not collapse the three material identities. Numerical P–V rows were not
available in the accessible primary record; the reproduction checks the three
parameterizations analytically at equal compression.
