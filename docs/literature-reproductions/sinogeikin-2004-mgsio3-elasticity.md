# Sinogeikin et al. (2004): ambient MgSiO3 perovskite elasticity

Primary source: S. V. Sinogeikin, J. Zhang, and J. D. Bass, *Geophysical Research Letters* **31**, L06620 (2004), DOI [10.1029/2004GL019559](https://doi.org/10.1029/2004GL019559).

## Audit result

The study measures single-crystal and polycrystalline Brillouin velocities at ambient conditions and compares aggregate elastic moduli. It contains no compression series or pressure-volume EOS fit.

- **REJECT — LitCurate source row 448.** The polycrystalline result combines an ambient conventional-cell volume of 162.4 Å³ with KS = 253(3) GPa, an adiabatic modulus derived from sound velocities. It is not a BM/Vinet P-V parameter set.
- **REJECT — LitCurate source row 449.** The single-crystal result uses the same ambient volume and derives the same aggregate KS from the measured elastic tensor. It is a second measurement geometry, not an independent EOS.
- **REJECT — LitCurate citation row 450.** KS = 264 GPa is the comparison value calculated from the single-crystal constants of Yeganeh-Haeri (1994), not a source result of this paper.
- **REJECT — LitCurate citation row 451.** The contextual literature-average static-compression value has no complete model or coefficients in this paper and is not attributable to Sinogeikin et al. (2004).

## Reproduction status

No production record was added. Treating an ambient adiabatic aggregate modulus as an isothermal pressure-volume bulk modulus would conflate distinct measurements.
