# Satta et al. (2025): ambient elasticity of Fe-bearing delta-AlOOH

Primary source: N. Satta, G. Criniti, T. Boffa Ballaran, A. Kurnosov, T. Ishii, J. Buchen, and H. Marquardt, *Physics and Chemistry of Minerals* **52**, 18 (2025), DOI [10.1007/s00269-025-01319-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00269-025-01319-7).

## Audit result

This open-access study combines ambient-condition single-crystal X-ray diffraction and Brillouin scattering to determine elastic stiffness tensors. It does not report a pressure-volume EOS.

- **REJECT — LitCurate citation rows 1268–1272.** These five rows reproduce bulk moduli or assumed pressure derivatives for AlOOH, Fe-bearing AlOOH, and epsilon-FeOOH from earlier compression studies. They are not results of Satta et al. (2025) and must be audited against their underlying primary publications.
- **REJECT — LitCurate source row 1273.** For sample Fe6, the reported V0 = 57.272 Å³ and KVRH = 161.0(22) GPa are an ambient unit-cell volume and a Voigt–Reuss–Hill aggregate modulus derived from the measured elastic tensor. No K' or P-V fit was measured, so they cannot define a Peritheos volume EOS.
- **REJECT — LitCurate source row 1274.** For sample Fe13, the reported V0 = 57.938 Å³ and KVRH = 150(1) GPa have the same ambient-elasticity meaning and are not EOS coefficients.
- **REJECT — LitCurate citation rows 1275–1276.** The quoted single-crystal aggregate moduli for Fe-free and lightly Fe-bearing delta-AlOOH come from Wang et al. (2022) and Satta et al. (2024), respectively. They are contextual comparisons, not new EOS fits in the 2025 paper.
