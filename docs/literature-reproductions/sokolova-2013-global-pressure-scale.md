# Sokolova et al. (2013) eleven-marker pressure-scale audit

## Conclusion

The eleven catalog records are no longer reported as if nothing numerical can
be checked. A coupled, source-constrained reconstruction is executable, but an
independent refit of the eleven EOS coefficient sets is still impossible from
the published evidence. The distinction is intentional:

- **Reconstructed:** the common ruby Equation (20) is evaluated against 392
  source-linked room-temperature observations for all eleven markers in one
  objective using the pointwise average of the precursor Table 2 Holzapfel and
  Table 3 Vinet isotherms. The published `A=1870 GPa, m=6.0` scale has a
  marker-equal RMSE of 1.2775 GPa. Letting only those two shared coefficients
  vary gives `A=1869.3838 GPa, m=6.103348` and 1.2639 GPa. The post-calibration
  Table 4 Holzapfel curves close against the published scale at 1.1474 GPa.
- **Not independently refitted:** Table 4 `K0`, `K0'`, and the thermal
  coefficients remain published parameters. The source does not provide the
  complete thermochemical and ultrasonic rows, residual weights, final
  optimizer settings, or covariance required to recover them.

## Published optimization reconstructed from the sources

[Dorogokupets and Oganov (2007)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.75.024115)
describe a two-stage weighted least-squares procedure. Stage 1 fits data not
dependent on a static pressure calibration: ambient thermochemical,
dilatometric and ultrasonic measurements plus shock Hugoniots. Its explicit
constraint is that oscillator multiplicities sum to `3n`. Stage 2 adds static
compression data on their original pressure scales, refits all parameters, and
checks the result against ambient-pressure adiabatic bulk moduli.

[Sokolova et al. (2013)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rgg.2013.01.005) apply that
method as follows:

1. Table 1 fixes the reference molar volumes, densities, linear/quadratic
   `Us-Up` shock coefficients, ambient `KS`, its adiabatic pressure derivative,
   and effective atomic number for diamond, MgO, Al, Cu, Nb, Mo, Ag, Ta, W, Pt,
   and Au.
2. Tables 2 and 3 give independently optimized Holzapfel and Vinet isotherms.
3. The eleven marker comparisons are averaged into the shared ruby equation
   `P = 1870 s (1 + 6 s)`, where `s = delta_lambda/lambda0` and
   `lambda0 = 694.24 nm`.
4. Static isotherms are corrected to that common ruby scale. The thermodynamic
   optimization is repeated with the Table 4 room-temperature `K0` and `K0'`
   fixed.

The publication calls the fits weighted least squares but never specifies the
per-observable weights or normalization. It plots many of the comparisons
rather than tabulating the selected rows. Therefore an exact numerical copy of
the full objective cannot be reconstructed without inventing information.

## Machine-readable comparison observations

The audit located a defensible comparison series for every marker. These are
not asserted to be the complete private input database. Published pressures
are inverted through their stated ruby calibration to recover the common
measured coordinate `s`; MgO supplies `s` directly.

| Marker | Dataset | Selected rows | Source pressure coordinate |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Diamond | `diamond_dewaele_2008_table1_pvt` | 12 | Holzapfel (2005), 298 K ruby rows |
| MgO | `mgo_jacobsen_2008_table1_helium_compression` | 45 | printed relative R1 shift |
| Al | `aluminum_dewaele_2004_table1_compression` | 40 | Mao et al. (1986) |
| Cu | `copper_dewaele_2004_table1_compression` | 42 | Mao et al. (1986) |
| Nb | `niobium_takemura_2006_table1_compression` | 43 | Dewaele et al. (2004), identified by source Table II |
| Mo | `molybdenum_dewaele_2008_table2_compression` | 28 | Mao et al. (1986) |
| Ag | `silver_dewaele_2008_table2_compression` | 33 | Mao et al. (1986) |
| Ta | `tantalum_dewaele_2004_table1_compression` | 36 | Mao et al. (1986) |
| W | `tungsten_dewaele_2004_table1_compression` | 42 | Mao et al. (1986) |
| Pt | `platinum_dewaele_2004_table1_compression` | 35 | Mao et al. (1986) |
| Au | `gold_dewaele_2004_table1_compression` | 36 | Mao et al. (1986) |

The reconstruction fixes every Table 1 marker constant, evaluates each Table 2
Holzapfel and Table 3 Vinet pressure at every selected volume, and minimizes the
sum of the eleven per-marker mean squared residuals against their pointwise
mean. Equal total marker weight implements the paper's statement that the ruby
scale averages the eleven marker scales without letting a longer table
dominate. This normalization is a transparent reconstruction choice, not a
claimed recovery of unpublished source weights. Bounds are
`1500 <= A <= 2200 GPa` and `0 <= m <= 20`. The fixed Table 4 Holzapfel curves
then provide the source's separate post-calibration closure check.

The fitted sensitivity solution changes `A` by -0.033% and `m` by +1.722%.
The small reduction in objective shows that the published Equation (20) is
consistent with the available comparison data at their printed precision; it
does not establish coefficient parity for the full thermodynamic fit.

## Official 2016 workbooks

The eleven publisher supplements to
[Sokolova et al. (2016)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cageo.2016.06.002) were
downloaded from the article's `mmc1.xls` through `mmc11.xls` deposits and
checked by SHA-256. They reproduce the material mapping, final constants,
named formulas, forward pressure calculation, and inverse volume calculation.
They are calculators, not optimization workbooks: no Solver model, residual
weights, covariance, or linked observation table is present.

Every workbook also contains the same unreferenced `SWDB`/`KK2002` block on a
`Graphs` sheet. The value block has the same normalized hash for all eleven
files and no formula in the calculators references it. Treating that copied
template block as eleven material-specific data sets would be scientifically
indefensible, so it is recorded in the manifest and excluded.

The MgO workbook stores the corrected negative anharmonic coefficient
`a0=-17.4e-6 K^-1`, while the 2013 Table 4 prints the opposite sign. That known
implementation correction remains separately traced in the catalog record and
does not affect the room-temperature ruby reconstruction.
