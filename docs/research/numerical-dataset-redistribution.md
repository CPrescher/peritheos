## What was reviewed

All 405 previously omitted material entries were screened using their preserved
references, table/figure locators, provenance, rights notices and actual numerical
content. Text columns were checked for labels and prose. The normalized resources
contain quantities, uncertainty fields, run/phase/source identities and short
contributor diagnostics. Longer explanatory notes were checked against contributor
curation scripts or diagnostic provenance. Source PDFs, artwork, captions,
original workbook layout and author software are not newly bundled.

This is **not a fresh source-value transcription audit**. No numerical values,
resource bytes, uncertainty meanings, digitized/calculated classifications,
scientific holds or source declarations were changed. The machine-readable
[review ledger](../data/numerical-redistribution-review.json) records every exact
declaration hash, resource checksum, source, locator, content profile, decision
and rationale. The package policy binds that entire ledger by SHA-256; new
assessed entries also bind their declaration and resource descriptors. Installed
packages carry the ledger, exact manifest and scoped numerical-transcription notice.

## Basis and limits

The admissions assess the distributed numerical content separately from source
expression. Article licensing is recorded rather than automatically applied to
all extracted facts. Existing contributor CC0 dedications cover only the explicitly held and
dedicated transcription, normalization, metadata and arrangement rights; missing
contributor grants are not created and third-party source rights are not relicensed. **Unspecified source terms remain unspecified.** Numerical admission
is not an author permission, a CC BY grant or a finding that every scientific
table is unprotected.

Scientific tables can contain protected expression, and collections can have
separate rights. Normalization alone does not remove database rights. There is
no universal row-count safe harbor. Identified historical compilations and large
complete collections are therefore held for a separate assessment, including any
independent investment in obtaining, checking and presenting their contents.
Source access and numerical validation do not establish redistribution permission.
Acquisition contracts were not exhaustively audited. Downstream distributors can
use the installed policy and ledger to distinguish source grants from factual
assessments and make narrower selections.

The framework is the [Creative Commons database FAQ](https://creativecommons.org/faq/#which-components-of-databases-are-protected-by-copyright),
[UrhG §2](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__2.html),
[UrhG §87a](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__87a.html), and
[CJEU C-203/02, paragraphs 31–35](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=celex:62002CJ0203).
Only official indexed excerpts of the judgment were accessible; its live full-text
endpoint returned an access challenge. These sources inform the assessment and
do not give approval for individual Peritheos resources. Debian must apply its
own [copyright policy](https://www.debian.org/doc/debian-policy/ch-archive.html#copyright-considerations).

not an independent measurement or an author-owned literature grant. The original
DAT's separate 100-point calculated curve and original author files are not added.
The recorded author permission was not independently authenticated in this review.
A fresh Nature/PMC request returned a challenge page, which is not license evidence.

**Anzellini (2025) iridium.** The final page of the freshly inspected
[primary article PDF](https://www.nature.com/articles/s43246-025-00963-4.pdf)
identifies CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. Both the 122-row supplement transcription and
107-point quantitative vector readout remain held pending a source-specific
applicability assessment or separate grant. This new source qualification updates
the local inventory only. It does not establish that every numerical value is
protected or inherits the article restriction.

**Other held cases.** Sakai/Ismailova NC/ND cases remain held rather than using a
blanket factual exemption. The two historical compilations require checking
upstream collection rights. The Shen/Smith simultaneous-volume workbook and
Campbell buffer-results collection require a collection-specific assessment;
the latter are computed outputs, not measured observations. The two Morard mixed
cold-data inputs need their original studies identified and assessed; author-file
permission does not clear the unidentified literature inputs. These holds do not
remove evidence from the research checkout.

are bound independently; unreviewed, held and mismatched entries are rejected.

bindings. Core-only checks retain 234 material documents and 685 source EOS records.

resource bytes preserved and no original PDF, image, workbook or author-code
artifact included. These are packaging and preservation checks, not experimental
validation or legal clearance.
