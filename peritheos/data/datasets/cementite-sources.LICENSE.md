# Fe3C source-table attribution and rights

Takahashi et al. (2019), *Thermal Equation of State of Fe3C to 327 GPa and
Carbon in the Core*, Minerals 9, 744, doi:10.3390/min9120744, distributes its
article under CC BY 4.0. The official supplementary workbook is preserved as
a factual transcription in `cementite-takahashi-2019-table-s1.csv` and the
raw-cell JSON. Original cell values, last-digit error tokens, number formats,
row identities and footnote attribution remain available. Changes consist
of machine-readable transcription, explicit missing cells and expansion of
printed error digits; pressures were not recalibrated.

Litasov et al. (2013), *Thermal equation of state and thermodynamic properties
of iron carbide Fe3C to 31 GPa and 1473 K*, JGR Solid Earth 118, 5274–5284,
doi:10.1002/2013JB010270, Table 1, is the authority for
`cementite-litasov-2013-table1.csv` and the original-token JSON. The table
reports 1σ errors. Source reuse terms beyond access to the paper were not
established and remain unspecified.

Prescher et al. (2012), *Structurally hidden magnetic transitions in Fe3C at
high pressures*, Physical Review B 85, 140402(R), doi:10.1103/PhysRevB.85.140402,
is the authority for the 19-row Table S1 CSV, raw xyzU tokens and cyclically
transformed structural states. The final supplement was supplied by the user.
Source reuse terms remain unspecified. Changes are factual transcription,
expansion of printed widths and an explicitly documented axis permutation;
no missing reference volume or uncertainty convention is inferred.

To the extent they hold such rights, Peritheos contributors dedicate only
their transcription, normalization, metadata and arrangement rights to CC0.
This does not relicense the publications, its figures, publisher PDFs or
third-party material. No publisher PDF is redistributed in these datasets.
