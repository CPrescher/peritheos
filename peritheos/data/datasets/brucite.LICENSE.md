# Brucite numerical source data and contributor rights

The numerical observations in `brucite-fukui-2003-table1.csv`,
`brucite-fukui-2003-table4.csv`, `brucite-xia-1998-table6.csv`, and the original
printed tokens and parameter metadata in `brucite-source.json` are factual
transcriptions attributed to:

- Fukui, Ohtaka, Suzuki and Funakoshi (2003), *Physics and Chemistry of
  Minerals* **30**, 511-516,
  [doi:10.1007/s00269-003-0353-z](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00269-003-0353-z).
  The final article states copyright Springer-Verlag 2003. No separate source
  numerical-data licence was identified; source terms remain **unspecified**.
- Xia, Weidner and Zhao (1998), *American Mineralogist* **83**, 68-74,
  [doi:10.2138/am-1998-0107](https://doi.org/10.2138/am-1998-0107).
  No separate source numerical-data licence was identified; source terms remain
  **unspecified**.
- Zigan and Rothbauer (1967), *Neues Jahrbuch fuer Mineralogie, Monatshefte*,
  137-143. `brucite-zigan-1967-cod-1000054.cif` preserves the
  [COD archival file](https://qiserver.ugr.es/cod/1000054.cif) verbatim, including
  its statement that data were placed in the public domain by contributors.
  That archive statement does not establish the rights in the original paper.
- He, Xuejing (2023), *Hydrogen-bonding configurations of magnesium
  hydroxyfluoride and fluorine-doped magnesium hydroxide under high pressure*,
  doctoral thesis, University of Tokyo, Table 4.3, pp. 81-82,
  [institutional repository](https://doi.org/10.15083/0002012205).
  `brucite-he-2023-table43.cif` is a contributor transcription of the
  fluorine-free, hydrogenated sample. The thesis became public in 2025;
  source numerical-data terms remain **unspecified**.
- Duffy et al. (1995), *American Mineralogist* **80**, 222-230,
  [doi:10.2138/am-1995-3-403](https://doi.org/10.2138/am-1995-3-403),
  for phase annotations; source numerical-data terms unspecified.
- Wu et al. (2026), *Molecules* **31**, 1631,
  [doi:10.3390/molecules31101631](https://doi.org/10.3390/molecules31101631),
  for phase annotations; original article is CC BY 4.0. Its numerical findings
  and unresolved text inconsistencies are attributed in the source manifest.

To the extent they hold rights in their own transcription, normalization,
metadata and arrangement, Peritheos contributors dedicate those rights under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). This dedication
does not cover third-party source rights, article text, figures, publisher
PDFs, or the original crystallographic publication. The original source terms
are not relabeled CC0. No missing or unspecified source licence was used as a
reason to omit recoverable factual observations.

Downloaded papers and rendered pages under `output/material-candidates/brucite/`
are local audit working copies. They are not redistributable data resources
under this dedication and are not part of the proposed installed material.
