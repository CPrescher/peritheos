## Independent Campbell NNO pressure contribution

`campbell-2009-independent-nno-pressure-integral.csv` evaluates Eqs. (5)-(6)
and printed Table 1 Ni/NiO parameters of Campbell et al. (2009),
DOI 10.1016/j.epsl.2009.07.022. It contains 1717 newly calculated relative
pressure contributions on the stated 0-100 GPa, 1000-2600 K calculation grid.
The gas constant, unit conversion, Debye law, reference temperature, quadrature
and input parameters are explicit in the declaration. No original output rows,
polynomial approximations or Knacke thermochemical table are generator inputs.
No open source-workbook or Knacke grant is asserted.

The output is delta log10 fO2 relative to mathematical zero GPa. It is NOT
absolute fugacity, a recovered 1-bar Gibbs reference, a complete Table S5
regeneration, experimental observations or a validated replacement EOS. The
source-relative increments differ by up to 0.0142591277 log units; the cause
is unresolved and no parameters are tuned to erase it. Exact absolute NNO
regeneration still requires Knacke Gibbs inputs/extrapolation; IW also requires
Chase extrapolation and the exact source iron phase-switch implementation.
Existing EOS dispositions, coefficients and original evidence remain unchanged.
