# Brown NaCl recovery: rights and provenance

The numerical measurements, published coefficients, calculated tables, and
transcriptions retain the source attribution specified in the accompanying
source manifest and research report. No third-party publication, figure,
table layout, PDF, or author-created dataset is relicensed here.

To the extent the contributors hold rights in their new transcription,
normalization, and metadata, those contributions are dedicated under CC0 1.0.
This statement makes no claim about third-party source rights. The executable
reconstruction scripts follow the repository's code license.

Recovered observations are distinguished from published reductions, polynomial
samples, calculated reference tables, and new reconstruction outputs. The
source CSV files must not be silently replaced with fitted or corrected values.
