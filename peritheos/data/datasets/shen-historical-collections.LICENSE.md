## Shen and Smith

The paper describes creation of the observations in two experiments, including
peak fitting, lattice-volume determination, uncertainties and pressure-drift
checks. The flat CSV contains quantitative results and ordinary run/phase/sequence
labels. It does not copy the source sheet layout, article prose, artwork or code.
The assessment distinguishes experimental data creation from independently
qualifying database investment. No separate substantial collection investment
independent of that creation was established from the inspected evidence.

This admits the complete numerical collection; it does **not** claim the
extraction is insubstantial or that all experimental databases are exempt.
Independent obtaining, verification or presentation investment could still
qualify. Acquisition/subscription contracts were not exhaustively audited.
Author affiliation, funding and public access are not treated as permission.
The final article still prints APS copyright; no separate open data grant was
found. A fresh workbook download was blocked; prior workbook verification is
distinguished from the current article/CSV inspection.

## Fixed historical excerpts

The historical assessments concern scientific numbers and functional ordering,
not book/article text, original table design, figures or captions. German
database-maker protection has a limited term and transitional rules: the fixed
1972 source precedes the 1983 transitional window, while any transitional term
for the fixed 1989 table has elapsed by 2026. This does not extinguish publication
copyright or establish worldwide public-domain status. No later substantially
reinvested database is extracted; later qualifying investment can create new rights.

The copper values are published **shock-reduced calculated 25 C isotherm**
entries, not direct static measurements. The original shock selection/reduction
remains unrecovered. The printed `09900` interpretation remains explicit, and
no blank or zero-pressure observation is invented. The full handbook was not
available for a fresh page/binary check; prior visual verification is retained.

Anderson combines Hultgren (1963), Barin/Knacke (1973), White/Collins (1972),
AIP (1972), Touloukian (1975), Neighbours/Alers (1958) and Chang/Himmel (1966).
The table contains literature properties, interpolation/smoothing and analytical
evaluations, not one common P-V-T measurement set. The two 300 K elastic branches,
blank fields and printed/reconstructed Cp distinction remain unchanged.
Identifying upstream citations does not claim fresh verification of every
upstream observation or reproduce the hand-smoothing procedure.
