# Sata (2010) Fe3C factual data and selected source rows

The Table 1 CSV files retain all nine Fe3C states, original tokens and paired
MgO readings from Sata et al. (2010), DOI 10.1029/2009JB006975. The Table 4 CSV
retains measured and calculated diffraction spacings as distinct columns.
The selected 12-row table adds only the three explicitly cited Li et al.
(2002) observations above 25 GPa, DOI 10.1007/s00269-001-0224-4. Those rows
overlap the existing Li dataset; they are not independent new measurements.
