# Fe-ringwoodite numerical transcriptions

The CSV files prefixed `ferrous-ringwoodite-` contain factual numerical
transcriptions, explicit normalization, or identified graphical checkpoints.
Attribution, locations, checksums, source-access limitations and uncertainty
conventions are recorded in `ferrous-ringwoodite-source.json`.

Source numerical-data licensing was not specified in the inspected articles.
This is not a claim that the articles, publisher files, figures or original
datasets are CC0. Armentrout and Kavner (2011) carries AGU copyright; Liu
et al. (2008) carries the publisher's research/teaching/private-study terms in
the coauthor-hosted copy. Preserve those notices and the source attribution.
The local PDFs and rendered pages under `output/material-candidates/` are
audit evidence, not assets covered by this dedication or proposed package data.

To the extent Peritheos contributors hold rights in their transcription,
normalization, metadata and arrangement, those contributor rights are dedicated
under CC0 1.0 Universal: <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>.
The dedication does not extend to rights held by third parties.

Suzuki (1979) values are calculated/extrapolated Fe-endmember results based on
Mao (1969), with confidence limits on the fitted solution. Figure 1 checkpoints
are digitized calculated curves. Neither is relabeled as an original numerical
observation table. Lattice-derived volumes and assumed mean temperatures are
explicit calculations, kept separate from reported quantities.

Stixrude-Lithgow-Bertelloni (2005) entries are published model parameters,
with reported uncertainties and systematics flags, not observations. The two
Armentrout (2012) Figure 9 points are digitized published residual symbols,
used only as graphical checks and never as fitting input.

Brown (1999) Tables I/II contain calculated calibration values, not additional
experimental observations. The supplied AIP PDF retains its copyright notice
and is local audit evidence only. The derived NaCl pressure replay retains both
published representations, their discrepancy, and explicitly marked thermal
extensions; it does not replace the original Table S1 pressures.
