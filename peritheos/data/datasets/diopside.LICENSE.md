# Diopside numerical transcription rights

The Zhao et al. (1998), DOI 10.4131/jshpreview.7.25, and Thompson and
Downs (2008), DOI 10.2138/am.2008.2684, numerical tables are attributed to
their original authors. No open reuse license for the source observations
was identified. Source rights remain unspecified; neither the articles nor
their publisher files, text, figures, or third-party rights are dedicated to
CC0 by Peritheos.

To the extent that Peritheos contributors hold rights in their factual
numerical transcription, normalization, metadata, and arrangement, those
contributor rights alone are dedicated under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The `*-original.txt` files retain the printed numerical notation and row
order. The CSV files expand parenthesized last-digit errors without assigning
an unreported confidence level. Blank errors mean unreported or constrained
by symmetry, as described in the material metadata; they do not mean zero.
The original Zhao table's duplicate ambient rows and inconsistent entries
are retained without correction in the source transcription. The separately
labelled `diopside-zhao-1998-table1-corrected-pvt.csv` applies the supported
Figure 2 pressure interpretation to row 15 while retaining the original
pressure and marking its graphical reading bound. Its accompanying README
explains the derivation and remaining source inconsistencies. Contributor
rights in this analysis correction are covered by the same limited dedication.
NaCl readings and Thompson ruby wavelengths are not tabulated in either source
and have not been synthesized.
