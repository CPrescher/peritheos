# Phase E numerical transcription: source terms and contributor rights

The original numerical observations and coefficients are attributed to
W. A. Crichton and N. L. Ross (2000), *Equation of state of phase E*,
Mineralogical Magazine 64, 561–567,
[doi:10.1180/002646100549427](https://doi.org/10.1180/002646100549427).
The article states copyright 2000 The Mineralogical Society; the publisher
identifies the Mineralogical Society of Great Britain and Ireland.
An explicit reuse licence for the source numerical data was not found.
Source licensing remains **unspecified**, not CC0.

Structural numerical facts are separately attributed to Kudoh et al. (1993),
Physics and Chemistry of Minerals 19, 357–360,
[doi:10.1007/BF00202972](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00202972), whose scan states
copyright Springer-Verlag 1993. Indexed phase E reflection identities are
from Shieh et al. (2000), American Mineralogist 85, 765–769,
[doi:10.2138/am-2000-5-616](https://doi.org/10.2138/am-2000-5-616).
No source numerical-data reuse licence was found for these transcribed facts.

The separately preserved author version is from Wilson Alexander Crichton's
2000 UCL PhD thesis, *Equations of State of Dense Hydrous Silicates*,
Table 6.1. Its reproduction states author copyright and all rights reserved,
with a separate ProQuest microform-edition notice. No source numerical-data
reuse licence was found; these source terms are retained in the manifest.
