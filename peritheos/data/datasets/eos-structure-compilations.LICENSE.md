# EOS parameters, crystal structures and compilation rights

Reviewed 5 November 2026. `eos-structure-rights-review.json` in the runtime
package binds this assessment to 685 EOS records and 234 structural/diffraction
components. It distinguishes numerical-content assessments, verified named
COD source declarations, absent structural values and unresolved collections.
It is a maintainer assessment, not a new source license or downstream approval.
The optional dataset package carries a copy for context; its observation
selection remains governed by its separate redistribution review.

## Numerical content and provenance

The assessed parameter records supply normalized scientific coefficients,
reported uncertainties, covariance, units, reference states, mathematical model
choices and applicability limits. The assessed structural records supply
lattice metrics, symmetry, coordinates, occupancies or explicitly identified
ideal/proxy models. Mathematical equations and numerical results are distinct
from source prose, source table layouts, figures and author software. Citations,
source locations and scientific qualifications must remain with these records.
This assessment does not authorize copying a publication, CIF commentary or
protected software implementation, nor does it treat a fit as measured data.

Contributor-written implementations and documentation retain MIT. The exact
upstream MIT notice for the pinned Dioptas material-library migration is in
`dioptas-materials.LICENSE.md`. That notice preserves rights held by the named
upstream contributors; it cannot clear third-party publications, inherited
card collections or acquisition agreements. Other original source terms remain
unspecified unless an exact applicable declaration is already recorded.
No new CC0 dedication or source grant is created here.

The named COD resources 1000054 (brucite), 9012770 (vanadium) and 9007379
(superhydrous phase B) retain the CC0 declaration documented in
`cod-cif-sources.LICENSE.md` and `cif-license-review.json`. Normalization,
cell scaling and the separately cited superhydrous-phase-B erratum stay
distinguishable from the archival CIF. Other inline COD identifiers are
provenance leads, not freshly verified resource matches or publication-wide
grants. EOS and observation rights are separate from each structure's terms.

## Unresolved compilation and acquisition cases

These components remain present in the runtime package. This review does not
silently remove or replace their numerical values, or claim an entirely
cleared source archive:

* **Seven Holland et al. (2013) modified-Tait records**, DOI
  `10.1093/petrology/egt035`: apv, mpv, cpv, fper, fpv, per and stv. The original
  four-coefficient isotherm extracts came from the official 256-endmember
  `tc-ds62.txt`. A fresh download matches the scientific audit's source hash.
  No explicit redistribution grant was found in that artifact or its bundle
  README. The official website describes academic availability and supports
  other implementations; neither establishes unrestricted redistribution of
  this extract. Collection/acquisition rights need a specific assessment or
  suitable permission. No THERMOCALC program, axfile or full dataset ships.
* **Ten inherited peak arrays**: fe3s, feh2, iceviii, majorite, mg7si2o8,
  mgfe60o, perovskite_orthorhombic, phase_d, sno2_cubic_27gpa and
  sno2_pa_3_at_48gpa. All 452 hkl/d/intensity rows exactly match their pinned
  Dioptas source arrays. Earlier acquisition/collection rights are not
  authenticated by Dioptas's MIT notice. A `.jcpds` filename alone does not
  establish ICDD/Powder Diffraction File origin or permission. Trace the
  original cards, obtain an applicable grant or independently regenerate a
  separately identified pattern from suitably sourced structures. Matching
  calculated lines alone would not recover measured source intensities.
* **Two ICSD-linked topology records**: mgsio3_post_perovskite and
  mg090fe009al0005ca0005sio3_post_perovskite. Their provenance cites Murakami
  et al. (2004), DOI `10.1126/science.1095932`, and ICSD 158959. The acquisition
  route is not authenticated. ICSD's published terms restrict reuse, including
  pattern collections; an ICSD citation does not prove that a particular
  contract applied to the present transcription. Establish independent primary
  or open-deposit lineage, or obtain permission. Isotropic cell scaling does
  not resolve the topology's acquisition rights. Coesite-V mentions ICSD in an
  unsuccessful search but contains no structural model; it is not one of these
  two cases.

The three newer, position-only fallback lists (two Nisr hydrous-silica records
and phase_e_fe0072) are separately assessed numerical hkl identities, calculated
d-spacings and uniform display markers. They are not inherited intensity
collections, experimental intensity measurements or complete atomic models.
Thirty-six materials have no lattice/sites/peak values to clear; that finding
does not grant rights in every other piece of their metadata.

## Assessment limits and downstream review

Individual numerical facts, protected expression, original selection or
arrangement, qualifying investment in obtaining/verifying/presenting a database
and acquisition contracts require separate consideration. Experimental or
computational data creation alone is not the same as obtaining existing
independent materials. That distinction does not eliminate all collection
rights, protectability differences between jurisdictions or contractual limits.
There is no automatic exemption based on a row count, scientific validation,
public access, a file extension or a change of format. Repeated/systematic
extractions and the combined source group matter as well as individual records.

For the remaining 678 EOS and 183 inline structural components, the review
records a scoped numerical-content assessment. It does not freshly verify every
primary publication, authenticate every contributor grant or audit historical
access agreements. Existing source terms and uncertainty/proxy/recommendation
qualifications remain intact. No observation-dataset license is extended to a
whole EOS record, material or structural model. The five held original dataset
entries remain held under the separate optional-package policy.

See the source report and primary-source links:
https://github.com/CPrescher/peritheos/blob/main/docs/research/eos-structure-compilation-rights.md

This notice preserves existing terms and documents unresolved actions; it is
not a substitute for permission where third-party rights actually apply.
