# Hu (2019) shock observations

The original dataset is **CC-BY-4.0**, as stated by its official PANGAEA deposit:

Fei, Yingwei (2019): *Shockwave hugoniot data for Fe3C* [dataset]. PANGAEA.
https://doi.org/10.1594/PANGAEA.905474

It supplements Hu, X., Fei, Y., Yang, J., Cai, Y., Ye, S., Qi, M., Liu, F., and
Zhang, M. (2019), *Phase Stability and Thermal Equation of State of Iron Carbide
Fe3C to 245 GPa*, Geophysical Research Letters 46, 11018–11024.
https://doi.org/10.1029/2019GL084545

License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Peritheos retains the original workbook and transcribes its numerical cells
into explicit tokens and typed columns. Normalization, uncertainty semantics,
the separate diagnostic fit, experimental coverage and possible-melting caveat
are documented in the audit. Original observations are unchanged. This dataset license does
not relicense the Wiley article, its text or figures.
