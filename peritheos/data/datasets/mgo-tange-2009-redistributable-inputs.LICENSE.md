# Redistribution notes for the Tange et al. input transcriptions

The two `mgo-*-cod-thermal-expansion.csv` files reproduce crystallographic
records from the Crystallography Open Database (COD). The COD states that its
data are dedicated to the public domain under CC0. Each row retains its COD
identifier so that the deposited record can be checked independently.

`mgo-marsh-1980-lasl-single-crystal-hugoniot.csv` transcribes the four quantities
read by the public-domain `hugoniot.f` program from the MgO single-crystal block
of `hugla.dat` in the Los Alamos Shock Hugoniot Data compendium, LA-4167-MS.

To the extent that Clemens Prescher and Peritheos contributors hold copyright,
related, or database rights in these CSV transcriptions—their normalization,
column names, and arrangement—they dedicate those rights to the public domain
under the [CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain
Dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

This dedication does not apply to the cited journal articles, their prose,
figures, typography, or other third-party material. The files are provided
without warranties.
