# Sakai, Ismailova and iridium: scope of numerical admission

Reviewed 5 November 2026. This notice concerns general redistribution of the
optional `peritheos-datasets` package. See installed `redistribution-review.json`,
`restricted_source_review`, for the exact 22 declarations/resources, hashes,
source evidence, content profiles and individual reasoning. Twenty-one are
admitted; Sakai Table S8 remains held. Admission is a maintainer assessment of
applicable rights, not a new source grant or a legal guarantee.

## Admitted content and reasoning

Sakai S1-S7 resources and the overlapping 326-row Au calibration subset contain
primary numerical experimental results and functional identities. Nine S9 grids
and the selected E/F/G columns of S14 contain mathematical numerical results,
not additional measurements. Ismailova contributes only four selected numerical
pressure/lattice/volume checkpoints. Iridium contributes 122 experimental rows
and 107 quantitative marker-center readouts; no source vector paths or image
pixels are included. The receipt documents each resource individually.

The assessment separates numerical facts/results from protected expression and
independent collection rights. It does not use the NC license to authorize
commercial use or the ND license to authorize sharing protected adaptations.
The source methods describe creating/processing measurements or evaluating
mathematical laws; no separate substantial collection investment independent
of creation was established for these admitted fields. Whole experimental-table
reuse is acknowledged. This is not proof that qualifying verification or
presentation investment is absent, a blanket experimental-data exemption, or
a row-count safe harbor. Acquisition contracts were not exhaustively audited.
Applicable third-party restrictions remain binding. A separate file-specific
CC BY 4.0/CC0 grant covering held rights would strengthen general reuse evidence.

No source prose, captions, artwork, workbook layouts/containers, PDFs or author
software is admitted. Sakai S8 remains excluded because its recent assembled
literature selection and calibration notes require further collection-rights
assessment or a suitable separate grant. Mere reformatting is insufficient.

## Scientific qualifications remain

Measured observations, digitized coordinates and calculated checkpoints remain
distinct. Sakai's 326-row aggregate overlaps individual measured tables. The
existing S9 first pressure is zero rather than workbook -0.0002242214081267206
GPa; original values and source evidence are preserved. Ismailova is a selected
checkpoint subset, not complete fit data; absent temperatures/calibrant inputs
are not filled. Iridium's graphical bounds are not experimental uncertainties;
printed Delta-T and calibrant blanks are retained. No coefficient, uncertainty
semantics, scientific classification or author-workflow claim is changed.

Retain attribution and these qualifications. The manifest/ledger enables
resource-specific exclusion where a distributor's assessment differs. Core
`peritheos` contains no observation values and is independently installable.
