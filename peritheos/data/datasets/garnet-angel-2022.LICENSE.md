# Angel (2022) garnet parameters and Wang (2019) grossular structure

Angel RJ, Gilio M, Mazzucchelli M, Alvaro M (2022), Garnet EoS: a critical
review and synthesis, Contributions to Mineralogy and Petrology 177, 54,
https://doi.org/10.1007/s00410-022-01918-5. The article and official
supplementary EOS parameter/covariance files are licensed CC BY 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Unchanged official supplements
1 and 2 are preserved in the repository docs/data/garnet-angel-2022/sources;
EOSMAT transcribes their pressure coefficients and EOS covariance submatrix,
converts only volume units, and adds explicit provenance and review findings.
The fixed molar V0 is attributed by Angel to Holland and Powell (2011); this
notice does not assert a general license for that upstream data collection.

Wang Y, Sun Q, Duan D, Liu X, Bao X (2019), The Study of Crystal Structure on
Grossular–Andradite Solid Solution, Minerals 9, 691,
https://doi.org/10.3390/min9110691. The article declares CC BY 4.0. Grossular's
nominal atomic sites and measured quenched lattice parameter are transcribed
from Tables 3 and 5, with measured-composition limitations from Table 4.

Source licenses are retained; this notice does not relicense third-party
article text, source figures, software, EosFit executables, or unrelated
upstream observations. Calculated comparison points are identified separately
from observations. No CC0 dedication is applied to these source files.
