Related publication: T. Katsura (2022), *A Revised Adiabatic Temperature Profile
for the Mantle*, Journal of Geophysical Research: Solid Earth 127,
e2021JB023562, https://doi.org/10.1029/2021JB023562.

recorded cell addresses, formulas and cached values in JSON, and supplied
